# Ad Network Research — BibleSpeak.org
_Researched: June 2026_

## Why Google AdSense Underperforms on Religious Content

Google classifies religion as a **sensitive personalized advertising category** — advertisers are prohibited from targeting users based on religious beliefs. On pages Google identifies as religious content, fewer advertisers can bid, resulting in lower CPMs and fill rates. One major Christian publisher (Concordia Publishing House) reported Google blocked their ads citing "faith expressed on the website."

**Bottom line:** AdSense will work but will earn 50–70% less than a comparable secular site. The fix is switching to a managed network that either has Christian-specific advertisers or doesn't apply the same restrictions.
